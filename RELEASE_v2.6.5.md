# Sheep Weather v2.6.5 🐑🌤️

An intuitive, ad-free weather companion for Android featuring dynamic character outfit forecasts and a customizable Samsung One UI-style Weather & Clock home screen widget.

---

## 📥 Quick Download & One-Touch Install
- **APK File**: `SheepWeather-v2.6.5.apk` (Attached below in Assets)
- **Supported Android Versions**: Android 10 (API 29) through Android 16 & 17+
- **Installation**: Download the APK to your Android device, tap the file, and tap **Install**. (If prompted, enable "Install unknown apps" for your browser or file manager).

---

## 🌟 What's New in Version 2.6.5

### 📱 Samsung-Style Weather & Clock Home Screen Widget
- **Weather & Clock Combined Layout**: Displays live local time (`TextClock`), formatted date, current location, temperature, condition icon, and high/low ranges.
- **Customizable Appearance**:
  - **Background Colors**: Pure Black or Clean White cards.
  - **Dark Mode Matching**: Optional dynamic sync with system light/dark theme.
  - **Transparency Slider**: Custom background opacity from 0% to 100% with instant presets (0%, 30%, 50%, 100%).
- **Interactive Configuration Screen**: Live widget preview screen modeled after Samsung One UI widget settings.
- **Direct App Launch**: Tap anywhere on the widget to open Sheep Weather directly to that location.
- **Quick Settings Cog**: Direct shortcut on the widget to reconfigure colors and transparency at any time.

### 🎨 Clean UI & Usability Refinements
- **Clutter-Free Main Screen**: Last updated timestamp and Data Saver mode status moved into the detailed location info sheet.
- **Smart Refresh Indicator**: The refresh icon button turns **vibrant green** when current weather data is more than 1 hour old, providing a clear visual cue to update.
- **Streamlined Forecast Cards**: Removed redundant "Next few hours" labels from outfit and layer forecast sections.
- **Updated Version**: Build updated to `v2.6.5` (versionCode 6).

## 🐑 About Sheep Weather

Sheep Weather re-imagines daily weather checking with personality and practical clothing advice:

- **Playful Character Forecasts**: A charming vector sheep that changes clothing depending on current conditions—wearing a raincoat in downpours, a cozy winter beanie and scarf in freezing cold, sunglasses on sunny afternoons, or an umbrella on stormy days.
- **Smart Layer Advice**: Actionable outfit suggestions (e.g., "Light shirt & shorts", "Medium jacket recommended", "Bring an umbrella") so you always know what to wear before stepping outside.
- **Detailed Meteorologic Telemetry**: Real-time temperature, "Feels Like", humidity, wind speed, UV index, and high/low forecasts powered by Open-Meteo.
- **Multi-Location Management**: Search and save your favorite cities with offline SQLite caching powered by Room database.
- **100% Ad-Free & Privacy Focused**: No trackers, no advertisements, zero clutter.
