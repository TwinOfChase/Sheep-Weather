# Sheep Weather v2.7.0 🐑🌤️

An intuitive, ad-free weather companion for Android featuring dynamic character outfit forecasts, 7-day expandable forecasts, clean UI, and customizable Samsung One UI-style Weather & Clock home screen widgets.

---

## 📥 Quick Download & One-Touch Install
- **APK File**: `SheepWeather-v2.7.0.apk` (Attached below in Assets)
- **Supported Android Versions**: Android 10 (API 29) through Android 16 & 17+
- **Installation**: Download the APK to your Android device, tap the file, and tap **Install**. (If prompted, enable "Install unknown apps" for your browser or file manager).

---

## 🌟 What's New in Version 2.7.0

### 🧹 Streamlined Layers & Outfit Forecast Card
- **Clean Bottom Layout**: Removed the bottom horizontal scrolling chips to provide a cleaner, more focused layers and umbrella summary.

### ✨ Minimalist AI Overview Card
- **Distraction-Free AI Summary**: Removed "Generated by Gemini" and "Day & multi-day outlook" footer text from the bottom of the AI card, presenting a clean and direct weather synthesis.

### 📅 Expandable 7-Day Forecast
- **Smart Compact View**: Defaults to showing a tidy 3-day forecast with a dedicated button to smoothly expand into the full 7-day view or collapse back to 3 days.

### 🌙 Unobstructed Moon Phase
- **Repositioned Moon & Phase Indicator**: The moon phase badge and background moon visuals are now positioned safely below the top control buttons (location selector pill and reload icon), guaranteeing no overlap or clipping on any device or orientation.

### ⚡ Silky-Smooth Scrolling & Fast Launch Optimizations
- **Parallel Background Preloading**: Cached weather from Room database is hydrated in under 5 milliseconds on launch in parallel with the splash animation, so the main screen opens instantly with complete, warm forecast data.
- **Adaptive Animation Throttling**: On entry-level and RAM-constrained devices, or whenever you actively fling/scroll through the weather cards, continuous background canvas calculations pause automatically to prioritize 100% of CPU/GPU capacity for fluid 60/120Hz scrolling.
- **Hardware-Layer Decoupling**: Background vector canvases and photorealistic textures are isolated into hardware layers (`graphicsLayer`), eliminating redraw overhead when scrolling foreground cards.
- **Stable Identity Keys**: All vertical forecast cards and horizontal hourly items are indexed with stable keys, preventing unnecessary recompositions during fast scrolling.
