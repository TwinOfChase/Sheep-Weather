# Sheep Weather v2.7.1 🐑🌤️

An intuitive, ad-free weather companion for Android featuring dynamic character outfit forecasts, 7-day expandable forecasts, clean UI, and customizable Samsung One UI-style Weather & Clock home screen widgets.

---

## 📥 Quick Download & One-Touch Install
- **APK File**: `SheepWeather-v2.7.1.apk` (Available directly in the root directory / GitHub release)
- **Supported Android Versions**: Android 10 (API 29) through Android 16 & 17+
- **Installation**: Download the APK to your Android device, tap the file, and tap **Install**. (If prompted, enable "Install unknown apps" for your browser or file manager).

---

## 🌟 What's New in Version 2.7.1

### 📍 Reordered Locations Drawer & Saved Cities
- **Saved Cities Priority**: The Saved Cities section is now placed at the top of the slide-out menu above settings, giving instant access to all saved locations.
- **Integrated Current Location**: "Use Current Location" (one-shot GPS) is now located directly inside the Saved Cities section right above your saved cities list.
- **Concise Update Button**: Shortened the action button label to `"Check For Updates"`.

### 🛡️ Dedicated Data Saver in Settings & Preferences
- **Clean Drawer Experience**: Removed the "Data Saver Active" banners from the slide-out drawer.
- **Settings & Preferences Badge**: "Data Saver Active" now appears exclusively within the **Settings & Preferences** menu alongside privacy toggles.

### 🚀 Updated Versioning
- Version bumped to **v2.7.1** (`versionCode 8`).
- Direct APK URL updated to `SheepWeather-v2.7.1.apk`.
