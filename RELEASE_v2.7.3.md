# Sheep Weather v2.7.3 🐑🎄✨

**100% Ad-Free, Privacy-First Weather Companion for Android Phones, Foldables, and Tablets**

Developed by **Pine Software Design Aotearoa**, Sheep Weather combines accurate real-time meteorological forecasts with a playful, helpful outfit guide—telling you at a glance how many clothing layers to wear, whether an umbrella is needed (or if it's too windy for one!), and how the weather shifts from sunrise to starry moonlit night. Designed for speed, offline reliability, and zero background data waste, Sheep Weather delivers a seamless experience across smartphones (`6.0"`–`6.7"`), foldables, and tablets (up to `10.4"`).

---

## 📥 Download & Installation
- **Latest APK**: `SheepWeather-v2.7.3.apk` (Attached below in GitHub Release Assets — universal adaptive build for phones, foldables, and tablets)
- **Compatibility**: Android 10 (API 29) through Android 16 & 17+
- **How to Install**:
  1. Download **`SheepWeather-v2.7.3.apk`** to your Android phone, foldable, or tablet.
  2. Open the downloaded `.apk` file from your browser downloads or Files app.
  3. Tap **Install** (if prompted, enable *"Install unknown apps"* for your browser or file manager).
  4. Launch **Sheep Weather**!

## ✨ Core Features of Sheep Weather
- **Morning Summary Notifications (On by Default)**: Daily morning weather briefing with customizable delivery time, showing current temperature, conditions, and daily high/low.
- **Adaptive Phone, Foldable & Tablet Support**: Single universal APK that dynamically detects screen dimensions up to `10.4"` while keeping `6.0"`–`6.7"` phone layouts untouched.
- **Two Signature Visual Styles**:
  - **Cartoon Sheep**: Animated sky and friendly sheep mascot whose eyes close peacefully to sleep after sunset (now with optional Santa hat & scarf!).
  - **Realistic Mode**: Crisp real-life photography transitioning into a glassy dark-sky veil, switching to realistic moon-phase imagery at night.
- **Layers & Outfit Forecast**: Instant layer count recommendation (`👕`), wind-aware umbrella alerts (*"Umbrella Needed"* / *"Too Windy for Umbrella"*), and practical clothing tips.
- **Gemini AI Weather Overview**: Concise, scannable daily briefing, upcoming-days outlook, and outdoor tips (can be toggled on/off anytime in Settings).
- **24-Hour & Expandable 7-Day Forecasts**: Quick 3-day default forecast with one-tap expansion to 7 days, plus a full Hourly Deep-Dive breakdown screen.
- **Home Screen Weather & Clock Widget**: Customizable Samsung One UI-style widget with Light/Dark/Auto themes and 0%–100% transparency control.
- **Offline-First & Data Saver**: Local Room database caching with zero silent background telemetry or hidden refreshes.
- **Android Auto Support**: Glanceable current conditions, hourly strip, and outfit recommendations safely formatted for your car display.
